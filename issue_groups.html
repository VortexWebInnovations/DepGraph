Issue groups on the Jira dependency graph

Group the issues on your Jira dependency map in 12 ways: epic or parent, project, team, assignee, sprint, fix version, status, status category, priority, issue type, dependency level, and level by open issues.

Grouping by project or team shows cross-project and cross-team dependencies. Grouping by dependency level shows the execution order: unblocked work first, then each step that waits for it.
